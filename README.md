
# CyberRisk-101 | Website Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

## Project Overview
A hypothetical cybersecurity risk assessment for ABC Online Services, a representative customer-facing website. This project identifies potential security risks, evaluates their likelihood and impact, and recommends prioritised mitigation strategies.

## Objectives
- Identify critical website assets and potential threats.
- Assess risks based on likelihood and impact.
- Prioritise security risks using a risk-scoring matrix.
- Recommend practical mitigation measures and assign risk owners.
- Estimate residual risks after proposed treatments.
- Document assumptions, methodology, and limitations.

## Risk Assessment Methodology
Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

| Score | Risk Level |
|---|---|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Medium |
| 10–16 | High |
| 17–25 | Critical |

The assessment uses qualitative ratings on a five-point scale and references the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0 and OWASP Top 10:2025.

## Key Findings
Ten candidate risk scenarios were assessed.

- **Injection:** 20/25, Critical
- **Broken Access Control:** 20/25, Critical
- **Cryptographic Failures:** 15/25, High
- **Authentication Failures:** 12/25, High
- **Security Misconfiguration:** 12/25, High
- **Software Supply Chain Failures:** 12/25, High
- **Logging and Alerting Failures:** 12/25, High
- **Insufficient Backup and Recovery:** 12/25, High
- **Denial of Service:** 12/25, High
- **Security Headers and Transport Configuration:** 9/25, Medium

## Project Deliverables
- **Project Scope and Assumptions:** Defines the hypothetical environment and assessment boundaries.
- **Research Notes:** Summarises the cybersecurity guidance used.
- **Excel Risk Register:** Contains risk descriptions, scoring rationales, treatments, owners, and residual-risk estimates.
- **Technical Report:** Documents the methodology, findings, recommendations, and limitations.
- **Supporting Evidence:** Relevant project screenshots and visual summaries.

## Limitations
This project is a hypothetical, desk-based assessment. No live website testing, penetration testing, or vulnerability scanning was performed. The identified risks are candidate scenarios, not confirmed vulnerabilities. Existing controls are unverified, and residual-risk scores are illustrative estimates.
